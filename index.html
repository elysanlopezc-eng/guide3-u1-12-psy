Theoretical Foundations: Fields of Psychology and Applications

Exploring professional sub-disciplines including clinical, developmental, educational, social, cognitive, health, industrial-organizational, and consumer psychology.

5-Min Warm-Up: Who might study shopping behavior?

Consider retail environments, digital storefronts, and brand loyalty. While marketing professionals analyze sales data, Consumer Psychologists and Industrial-Organizational (I-O) Psychologists investigate the underlying cognitive drivers and group dynamics that dictate commercial choices.

1. Clinical Psychology

Diagnoses and treats psychological disorders, emotional disturbances, and behavioral anomalies through evidence-based psychotherapy.

Application: Mental health clinics, hospitals, counseling.

2. Developmental Psychology

Examines human growth and psychological changes across the lifespan—from infancy through childhood, adolescence, adulthood, and aging.

Application: Toy design, educational milestones, lifespan research.

3. Educational Psychology

Studies how people learn, retaining instructional methods, teaching psychology, and addressing learning disabilities.

Application: Curriculum design, e-learning optimization, schools.

4. Social Psychology

Investigates how social environments, group dynamics, peer pressure, and cultural norms influence individual thoughts, feelings, and actions.

Application: Advertising influence, public policy, crowd behavior.

5. Cognitive Psychology

Focuses on mental processes such as attention, memory, language, problem-solving, and decision-making architecture.

Application: UX/UI design, AI interaction, memory research.

6. Health Psychology

Explores how biological, psychological, and social factors affect health, illness prevention, stress management, and medical compliance.

Application: Public health campaigns, wellness programs, hospitals.

7. Industrial-Organizational (I-O)

Applies psychological principles to workplace productivity, employee selection, leadership training, and organizational culture.

Application: Corporate HR, talent assessment, workflow design.

8. Consumer Psychology

Studies buyer habits, emotional triggers, advertising persuasion, and decision-making processes to optimize marketing strategies.

Application: Brand positioning, market research, ad campaigns.
